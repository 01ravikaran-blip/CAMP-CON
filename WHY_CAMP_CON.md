# 🎓 Why Join CAMP-CON?

Tired of chaotic social media? Welcome to **CAMP-CON** — the exclusive social network designed specifically for our university community.

## 🌟 Why This App?

### 1. 🏛️ University Exclusive & Verified
No bots, no fake news. Every user is verified with their university credentials. When you see a **Blue Tick**, you know it's a real student or faculty member. It's a safe space to discuss campus life, events, and academics.

### 2. 👻 Anonymous Mode (Ghost)
Want to share a confession or a hot take without attaching your name? Go **Ghost Mode**. Share your thoughts freely but responsibly—community guidelines still apply!

### 3. 📸 Advanced Media & Albums
Share your campus memories in high quality. Create albums, upload 4K photos, and record voice notes for those quick updates.

---

## 🚀 Gamified "Influence" System
Your activity matters! We don't just give you everything at once; you earn it by being a valuable member of the community.

**Unlock Features by Growing Your Influence:**
Your **Influence Score** is calculated based on your **Followers**, **Upvotes**, and **Reposts**.

| Tier | Features Unlocked |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Newbie** | ✅ Text Posts <br> ✅ Image Uploads <br> ✅ Voice Notes |
| **Rising Star** | 🔓 **Video Uploads (Reels)** <br> 🔓 **Create Albums** |
| **Influencer** | 🔓 **Long-form Videos** <br> 🔓 **Priority Search Ranking** |
| **Campus Icon** | 🔓 **GO LIVE! (Streaming)** <br> 🔓 **Verified Badge** <br> 🔓 **Moderation Powers** |

**How to Level Up?**
*   **Post quality content**: Get Upvotes (Red Hearts).
*   **Engage**: Comment and build a following.
*   **Be trusted**: Avoid Downvotes (and Community Notes on your posts!).

---

### 🛡️ Community Safety
We use a **Community Notes** system. If you spread misinformation, the community can attach a "Context Note" to your post. Democracy rules here!

**Ready to start your journey? Log in and start building your legacy today!** 🚀
